Engineering a Truly Human-Like Voice (Eliminating the "Robot" Feel)

Why traditional IVRs sound robotic—and the 5 technical switches we use so RAKEZ investors feel like they are speaking to a real person:

1. Expressive Neural Voice Models (Not Standard TTS)

Standard browser or legacy IVR voices read text flatly. In production, use Azure OpenAI `gpt-4o-realtime` Speech-to-Speech or Azure AI Speech Dragon HD / Neural HD Voices (`uaenorth`) (or ElevenLabs Flash v2.5 at ~75ms latency). These models naturally breathe, vary pitch on key words, and inflect upward on questions.

2. Sub-600ms Turn-Taking & Sentence-Chunk Streaming

Humans respond within 300–600ms. If an AI waits 3 seconds to generate a full paragraph before speaking, the caller immediately knows it's a bot. Our pipeline streams the first 5 words into TTS the instant the LLM emits a comma or period.

3. Conversational Backchanneling & "Filler" Masking During CRM Lookups

When calling `create_crm_ticket` or querying `Portal 360`, a robotic bot goes dead silent for 1.5 seconds. Our orchestrator immediately speaks a natural filler phrase ("Let me pull up your Portal 360 file real quick..." or "Got it, raising that request for you right now...") while the API call runs in the background.

4. Full-Duplex Interruption (Barge-In) & Subtle Ambient Office Comfort Noise

Dead digital silence between turns sounds artificial on a phone line. Mixing a -48dB subtle call-center room tone and halting TTS within 50ms when the investor interrupts (`Silero VAD`) makes the call indistinguishable from a live RAKEZ relationship manager.

Your Action Checklist to Make Real Calls from Your Personal Number

Everything in the simulator works immediately in your browser with zero setup. When you want to make your physical mobile phone ring using your personal number as Caller ID, complete these 4 quick steps on your laptop:

Step 1: Verify Your Personal Number on Twilio (5 mins) Action on You

Sign up for a free Twilio trial account, go to Phone Numbers → Manage → Verified Caller IDs, and enter your personal UAE mobile number (`+971 5x...`). Enter the 6-digit OTP you receive. You can also verify a second mobile number (e.g. a colleague's phone) to act as the RAKEZ Relationship Manager for warm transfers.

Step 2: Start an ngrok Tunnel on Your Laptop (2 mins) Action on You

Install `ngrok` and run ngrok http 8000 in your terminal. Copy the forwarding URL (e.g., wss://a1b2-c3d4.ngrok-free.app/ws/twilio-media-stream) so Twilio can stream real-time voice audio to your laptop.
