Why Speech-to-Text Was Fixed

Earlier, connecting the Gemini API key caused two things: (1) Gemini TTS audio played through an HTML5 player that wasn't muted when the microphone opened, causing speaker echo into your mic, and (2) browser speech recognition often mis-spelled UAE terms like "RAKEZ" (as "rackets") or "3-year" (as "free year").

Now Fixed: We added automatic speaker-echo muting before opening the mic, a UAE Phonetic Auto-Corrector for RAKEZ terms, and a hybrid router so local offer matching always runs at 0ms before calling Gemini.

How to Ring Any Phone Number Live During Your Demo

Step 1: Verify Target Number on Twilio Trial (2 mins)

In your free Twilio trial console, open Phone Numbers → Manage → Verified Caller IDs and verify your mobile number (`+971 5x...`). (On a paid Twilio account, you can dial any number without pre-verifying it.)
